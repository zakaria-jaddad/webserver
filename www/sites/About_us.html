SYSTEM ORIGIN LORE

OPERATION: POTATO SERVER

THE CRISIS: Kessoku Band desperately needed an online presence to stream their tracks, but traditional cloud platforms are expensive. Ryo spent the remaining band funds on vintage bass gear, and Bocchi had a panic attack trying to understand server pricing sheets.

THE FIX: Enter the Tech Crew. We hijacked an ancient, dust-caked potato computer forgotten in a dark corner of the STARRY Live House basement. Written completely by hand from scratch in raw C++98, this non-blocking, asynchronous HTTP/1.1 engine is fueled entirely by low-level system sockets and intense social anxiety. It’s scrappy, it costs zero yen to host, and it works!
